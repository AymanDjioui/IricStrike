identifiable by its name prefix:

  · License: `LICENSE.bughunter-CC-BY-4.0.txt` · Changes: none (verbatim).

  (SnailSploit), **MIT**. Source: https://github.com/SnailSploit/claude-red
  · License: `LICENSE.claude-red-MIT.txt` · Changes: 28 converted from claude-red's
  custom format to native `SKILL.md` (bodies preserved).
