SQLi, auth-bypass, SSTI, XXE, JWT, OAuth, SAML, GraphQL, race-condition,
business-logic, deserialization, request-smuggling, and more) so the engagement
workflow has real, report-derived methodology to hunt from. They are **guidance
only** — all active tool execution still passes through the IricStrike scope-guard.
